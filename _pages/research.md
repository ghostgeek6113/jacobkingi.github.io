## Image understanding under data scarcity

My current research interest is understanding images when data is limited. I am interested in how visual models can learn effectively from small datasets and limited annotations.

## Generative data augmentation

My publication on **controllable diffusion-based augmentation** explores generating synthetic X-ray images to support object detection. The work uses structural and class information to guide image generation and evaluates the approach in both standard and limited-data settings.
